Battery Management System (BMS) Design & Implementation

Role: Electronic Subgroup Member & Battery Officer at Solar Team Solaris

A comprehensive hardware and embedded software engineering project focused on ensuring the safe, efficient, and reliable operation of a 12-cell battery pack for a highly competitive solar vehicle.

C / C++ Altium / KiCad CAN-Bus / UART PCB Design STM32

The Challenge: Managing High-Stress Energy Systems

During competitive solar racing, battery packs are subjected to extreme charge and discharge cycles, unpredictable environmental conditions, and high mechanical stress. Relying on basic monitoring was insufficient for the safety and performance standards required. We needed a highly reliable way to capture the exact parameters—voltage, current, temperature, and State of Charge (SoC)—acting on 12 individual cells to prevent overcharge, over-discharge, and dangerous thermal runaways.

The Engineering Solution

To bridge the gap between basic power delivery and intelligent battery management, I engineered a custom Battery Management System (BMS) from the ground up. I designed multi-layer PCBs utilizing Altium Designer and KiCad to handle precision sensor integration and safe power distribution.

By strategically combining these custom boards with bare-metal C/C++ firmware, we successfully captured real-time battery data. I implemented robust CAN-Bus and UART communication protocols to stream this critical telemetry directly to the main vehicle control unit, enabling dynamic active and passive cell-balancing and ensuring peak operational safety.
